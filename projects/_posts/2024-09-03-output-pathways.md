The SCN sends timing signals through multiple downstream pathways, but we still know relatively little about how individual pathways control specific features of daily behavior. We identified a sparse population of SCN neurons that projects to the dorsomedial hypothalamus (DMH) and regulates the timing and duration of locomotor activity.

We now use this pathway as a model for understanding how circadian timing is transmitted and transformed between the SCN and downstream neural circuits. More broadly, we are interested in how different SCN output pathways control distinct behavioral and physiological rhythms.
